About CarbonTrack

Empowering individuals and communities to understand, track, and reduce their environmental impact.

🌍 Our Mission

Climate change is the defining challenge of our generation. At CarbonTrack, we believe that awareness is the first step toward action. Our platform provides the tools, data, and community support needed to make sustainable living accessible, measurable, and rewarding for everyone.

Our Values

The principles that guide everything we build.

🔍

Transparency

We use open, verified emission factors from EPA and DEFRA. Our methodology is clear, our data is trustworthy.

🤝

Community

Change happens together. Our challenges and leaderboards foster a supportive community of eco-conscious individuals.

🌱

Impact

Every small action counts. We help you see the real impact of your choices and celebrate every step toward sustainability.
